Memoryless Property¶

Definition¶

Memoryless: P(X≥s+t∣X≥s)=P(X≥t)\P{X\geq s+t\given X\geq s}=\P{X\geq t}P(X≥s+t∣X≥s)=P(X≥t). Under the standard support assumptions, the geometric distribution is the only nonnegative integer-valued memoryless distribution, and the exponential distribution is the only nonnegative continuous memoryless distribution.
